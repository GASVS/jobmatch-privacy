JobMatch AI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Publisher: Andrej Gorskolepov · Contact: gorskolepov.andrej@gmail.com

This policy applies to JobMatch AI version 1.0 ("the App"), a standalone, on-device career application tool. It is not legal advice.

Overview

JobMatch AI is a privacy-first career application tool. The standalone V1 release is designed to process career information on the user's device without an operator-run backend. The App contains no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and no advertising or tracking components.

Information stored on your device

The App may store the information you enter or create, including:

  • Career-profile information and CV/resume text
  • Job descriptions, job links, preferences, and application-tracker entries
  • Generated application materials, interview-preparation notes, and local credential records
  • App preferences such as language and theme

This information is stored locally in the App's device storage. The publisher does not operate a V1 server that receives or stores this information.

Information we collect

The standalone V1 App does not collect, sell, rent, or share your career information with the publisher. It does not require an account, job-board credentials, or job-platform cookies.

Optional third-party AI-provider settings

The App may let a user voluntarily enter their own API key for a third-party AI provider. Any such request is sent directly by your device to that provider under its own privacy terms; the publisher is not an intermediary. Do not enable any external provider unless you understand and accept that provider's terms.

Files and exports

CV files selected for local parsing and generated exports are processed on your device. When you choose Share, Save, Print, AirDrop, email, or another device sharing destination, the destination you select controls the subsequent handling of the exported material.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete local App information using Settings → Clear All Data. This removes JobMatch-owned stored records from the App/device storage. You can also uninstall the App, which removes the local data on most devices. Exports you saved or shared outside the App must be deleted separately from their chosen locations.

Children

JobMatch AI is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to consent to the use of this type of service. Do not submit children's personal information through the App.

Security

The App is designed to minimize data transmission by keeping V1 career data on-device. No method of local storage or device security is perfect. Protect your phone with a passcode/biometric lock and review exported files before sharing them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Material changes that introduce remote processing, analytics, accounts, a backend, or different data practices will be described before or when they take effect, as required by applicable law.
